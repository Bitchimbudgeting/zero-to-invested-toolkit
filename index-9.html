Everything you can buy in the stock market has a short code called a ticker. VOO is a ticker. You type it in, and this toolkit tells you what it is, what it costs, and whether it fits a beginner portfolio.

  1. 1Look something up. Tap one of the beginner picks below, or type any ticker.

  3. 3Compare and practice. Put two side by side, build a practice portfolio, and check for overlap.
  4. 4Explore long term growth, then see your next step at the bottom.
